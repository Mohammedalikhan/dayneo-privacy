1. Introduction

Dayneo is a simple, local todo app. This policy explains how the current version handles information. Dayneo stores tasks on your device and does not upload them to a Dayneo server.

2. Information We Collect

Dayneo does not collect task information or personal information off your device. There is no account system, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, tracking, or external crash-reporting service.

You may voluntarily type personal information into a task. Dayneo does not separately collect or transmit that information; it remains part of the task stored locally on your device.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

For each task, Dayneo stores the task text, completion status, creation timestamp, and a locally generated task ID. The task ID identifies a task within your list; it is not an advertising identifier or account ID.

Tasks are saved using AsyncStorage under the storage key @my_tasks_v1 in the app’s local storage. Dayneo does not use a task server or backend database.

App frameworks may also use local preferences, runtime information, and caches to support the interface. Dayneo does not transmit these as analytics or tracking data.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

Dayneo does not ask you to provide a name, email address, financial information, or an account profile. It does not collect browsing history, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics.

Dayneo does not request access to location, contacts, the camera, microphone, photos or the photo library, health data, Bluetooth, motion or fitness data, calendars, or reminders. It does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

5. How We Use Information

Locally stored task information is used only to display and manage your list, including creating, editing, completing, deleting, and clearing completed tasks, and restoring your list when you reopen the app.

Dayneo does not use your tasks for advertising, profiling, marketing, or training an AI system.

6. Data Sharing

Dayneo does not upload, sell, rent, or share your task information with a Dayneo server, advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

Dayneo does not provide cloud synchronization or an in-app task-sharing service. Copies you create outside Dayneo and operating-system services are outside the app’s control.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Tasks remain in local app storage until you delete them, clear completed tasks, or remove the app’s local data. Completing a task does not delete it.

Use the delete action to remove an individual task. Use Clear Completed to remove completed tasks. These actions update the saved local list; Dayneo has no server copy to delete.

Deleting the app, rather than merely offloading it, normally removes its local app data. Copies retained independently by you or by operating-system backup or restore services may follow different retention rules. Dayneo does not promise forensic or secure erasure.

Dayneo itself does not operate a backup or recovery service.

8. Security

Task storage is within the app’s local storage area and relies on the security protections provided by your device and operating system. Dayneo does not add its own encryption layer to AsyncStorage.

No storage method is completely secure. Protect your device with its available security settings and consider whether to enter sensitive information into a task.

9. Children’s Privacy

Dayneo does not collect personal information off the device, including information from children. It has no accounts, behavioral advertising, or tracking system.

Information typed by a child into a task is handled in the same local-only way described in this policy.

10. Third-Party Services

Dayneo uses software libraries such as React Native, Expo, and AsyncStorage to provide its interface and local storage. The current app does not integrate analytics, advertising, tracking, or an external crash-reporting service.

The presence of a framework capability does not mean Dayneo uses it to collect or upload tasks. Frameworks may access local app or device information needed for their operation.

Your device’s operating system and app store may process information under their own policies, separately from Dayneo’s task-storage implementation.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its privacy practices change. An updated policy will be made available in the app, with a revised last-updated date. This policy describes the current local-only implementation, not hypothetical future features.

12. Contact Us

For support or privacy questions, email dayneo.support@gmail.com.

If you choose to email us, the information you include is sent through your email service and our Gmail mailbox, separately from the app’s local task storage. Do not include sensitive task information.
